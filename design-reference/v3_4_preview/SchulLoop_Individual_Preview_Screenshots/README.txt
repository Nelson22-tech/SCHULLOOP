SchulLoop — Individual Preview Screenshots

This folder contains individual PNG crops of the available SchulLoop preview boards, grouped by platform module.

01_Global_School_Finder_and_Platform: 15 screens covering onboarding, school discovery, admissions, payments, profile, notifications, admin, and subscriptions.
02_Student_Connect_and_Portal: 10 screens covering communities, messages, group chat, student applications, receipts, and documents.
03_Job_Portal: 10 screens covering job search, listings, details, applications, saved jobs, messaging, and profile/settings.
04_Original_Preview_Boards: the original full-size preview images for reference.

Note: These are image crops from the supplied design boards, not screenshots captured from a running app.
